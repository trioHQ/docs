A **receivable** is money that arrives in one of your bank accounts: a dynamic QR code your customer pays, a boleto settled at any bank, or a Pix Automático collection debited on its due date. On V2 every receivable is created against a `bank_account_id`, the account that receives the funds, and becomes an **in document** once it is paid.

## Choose how to get paid

| You want to | Use | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charge a known amount right now | Dynamic QR code | [Cash in](/v2/guides/receivables/cash-in) |
| Let customers pay any amount, repeatedly | Static QR code | [QR code types](/v2/guides/pix/qr-codes) |
| Charge with a due date, discount, fine or interest | Dynamic QR code with due date, or a boleto | [QR code types](/v2/guides/pix/qr-codes), [Create a boleto](/v2/guides/boleto/create-boleto) |
| Issue a bank slip that also carries a Pix QR code | Boleto | [Create a boleto](/v2/guides/boleto/create-boleto) |
| Charge a subscription without asking each time | Pix Automático | [Pix Automático](/v2/guides/receivables/using-pix-automatic) |

## What happens after the payment

Trio records the payment as an in document, sends the webhook events you subscribed to, and credits the bank account. Read the [Document lifecycle](/v2/guides/pix/document-lifecycle) for the statuses, and [Webhooks](/v2/webhooks/introduction) for the events.
