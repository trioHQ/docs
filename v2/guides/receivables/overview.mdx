title: "Receivables"
description: "Cash-in guides for V2: receiving payments, fees, split payments, Pix Automático and sandbox simulations."
---

**Receivables**, or cash-in, are the payments that arrive in your bank accounts: a dynamic QR code that gets paid, a boleto settled by a customer or a Pix Automático collection debited on its due date. On the V2 API every receivable is created against a `bank_account_id` and produces an **in document** once it is paid.
