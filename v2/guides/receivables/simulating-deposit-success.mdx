---
title: "Simulate a successful deposit"
description: "Settle a sandbox deposit end to end by paying your own QR code from a second bank account."
---

To watch a deposit settle end to end in the sandbox you need two bank accounts: one that creates the QR code and receives the funds, and one that pays it. This guide creates the second account, generates a QR code on it and pays the code from the first account in the [Trio App](https://app.sandbox.trio.com.br). It builds on the [Cash in](/v2/guides/receivables/cash-in) tutorial.

All examples use the sandbox base URL `https://api.sandbox.trio.com.br`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a second bank account">
    {/* TODO(v2): verify — V1 created the secondary account as a Virtual Account in the console (Account > Virtual Accounts). V2 has no virtual accounts; the console steps and screenshots below are from V1 and need to be confirmed for V2. */}

    In the Trio App, open the menu and go to **Account > Virtual Accounts**. At the top right of the list, click **Create Virtual Account**.

    ![](/images/image-\(30\).png)

    Select your real bank account (typically there is only one) and give the new account a short description.

    ![](/images/image-\(31\).png)

    The new account appears in the list with its own `id` and balance. Your first account starts with BRL 1,000,000.00; the new one starts with BRL 0.00.

    Through the API, create the second account with [`POST /v2/bank_accounts`](/v2/api-reference/banking-api/bank-accounts/create-bank-account) (`group_id` and `description`) and list both, with their ids and balances, with [`GET /v2/bank_accounts`](/v2/api-reference/banking-api/bank-accounts/list-bank-accounts).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a QR code that points to the new account">
    Send a `POST` to [Create a dynamic QR code](/v2/api-reference/banking-api/receivables/qrcode-dynamic/post) with the new account's `id` in `bank_account_id`. The amount is in cents (BRL 100.00 = `10000`), so `5000` is a BRL 50.00 deposit.

    ```http
    POST /v2/in/pix/qrcodes
    ```

    ```json
    {
        "bank_account_id": "0196102e-f67b-d5f9-300c-f587094bfc5a",
        "counterparty": {
            "tax_number": "41.491.440/0000-11",
            "name": "My Test Company"
        },
        "amount": 5000,
        "external_id": "{{$randomUUID}}",
        "description": "Test Payment in Sandbox",
        "options": {
            "allow_change_the_amount_on_payment": false,
            "show_qrcode_image": false
        }
    }
    ```

    Keep the `hash` from the response: it is the BR code (the Pix copy-and-paste code) you pay in the next steps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Pix in the Trio App">
    The account with BRL 0.00 receives the deposit; your first account, which holds the balance, pays it. Go to **Move your account > Pix**.

    ![](/images/image-\(32\).png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the origin account">
    Choose the first account, the one with the full balance.

    ![](/images/image-\(33\).png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the BR code">
    Paste the `hash` returned by the API.

    ![](/images/image-\(34\).png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the payment">
    Follow the remaining confirmation screens. The deposit settles, Trio sends the [webhook event](/v2/webhooks/events/overview), and the transaction appears in your transaction list and in [`GET /v2/documents/in`](/v2/api-reference/banking-api/documents/receivables/list-receivables).

    ![](/images/image-\(35\).png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
