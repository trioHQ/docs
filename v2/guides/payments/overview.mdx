---
title: "Payments"
description: "Cash-out guides for V2: paying Pix keys, QR codes and bank accounts from your accounts."
---

**Payments**, or cash-out, are the transfers that leave your bank accounts. On the V2 API a payment is debited from the `bank_account_id` you inform and produces an **out document** whose status you can follow through webhooks or by reading the document.
