A **boleto** is a bank slip your customer can pay at any bank, and on Trio it is also a hybrid: every boleto carries a Pix QR code that settles instantly. On the V2 API a boleto is issued against a `bank_account_id`, can carry discounts, fines, interest and split rules, and becomes an **in document** when it is paid.
