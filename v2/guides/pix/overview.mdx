Pix is the instant payment method of the Central Bank of Brazil. On the V2 API, a Pix payment you receive settles into a bank account you own and is represented by an **in document**. The guides in this section cover how a payer reaches you through a QR code, what happens to the document after the payment, and how to validate the Pix keys your integration handles.
