Each notification carries a `category` and a `type` in the envelope described in [Content and structure](/v2/webhooks/content-and-structure). Use the same names in `event_categories_allowed` when you [register a webhook](/v2/webhooks/introduction#choose-which-events-you-receive) to receive only the events you need.

## Categories and types

{/* TODO(v2): verify — the V2 spec does not list the event categories; the table below is the V1 list and its names may differ in V2 (the spec example uses `boleto`, not `collecting_document`) */}

| Category | Types | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `boleto` | `created`, `settled` | A boleto you issued was created or paid. The only category the V2 spec names, in its webhook example. |
| `collecting_document` | `settled`, `failed` | A cash-in document (a payment you received) was registered or refused. |
| `collecting_document_refund` | `created`, `settled`, `failed` | A refund of a payment you received was created, settled or failed. |
| `payment_document` | `created`, `settled`, `failed` | A cash-out document (a payment you sent) was created, confirmed or declined. |
| `payment_document_refund` | `settled` | A refund of a payment you sent was settled. |
| `transfer_document` | `settled` | A transfer between your bank accounts was settled. |
| `recurrence` | `created`, `approved`, `rejected`, `expired`, `cancelled` | A Pix Automático recurrence changed state. |
| `recurrence_collection` | `created`, `active`, `settled`, `expired`, `rejected`, `cancelled` | A collection of a Pix Automático recurrence changed state. |
| `recurrence_collection_attempt` | `requested`, `scheduled`, `settled`, `cancelled`, `rejected`, `expired`, `failed` | An attempt to settle a collection changed state. |
| `initiation_document` | `created`, `awaiting_consent`, `awaiting_payment`, `settled`, `rejected`, `failed` | A payment initiated through Pix biometrics moved along its lifecycle. |
| `entity` | `in_review`, `approved`, `rejected` | The compliance status of an entity created through onboarding changed. |

<Note>
  V1 also documented a `pix_biometrics_enrollment` category. The V2 API has no Pix biometrics enrollment endpoints, so that category is not part of the V2 documentation.
</Note>

## Boleto

Category `boleto`, with the types `created` and `settled`. It is the category the V2 spec uses in its [Create webhook](/v2/api-reference/banking-api/developers/webhooks/create-webhook) example. See [Create a boleto](/v2/guides/boleto/create-boleto) for the flow that produces these events.

## Collecting document

Category `collecting_document`: a cash-in document, a payment you received. See [Cash-in](/v2/guides/receivables/cash-in) and [Document lifecycle](/v2/guides/pix/document-lifecycle).

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `settled` | A collecting document (cash-in) was successfully received and registered in the system. |
| `failed` | A collecting document (cash-in) was received but could not be processed and was refused. |

## Recurrence

Category `recurrence`: a Pix Automático recurrence, the authorization a payer gives you to collect from their account on a schedule. | --- | --- |
| `created` | A recurrence was created. |
| `approved` | A recurrence was approved. |
| `rejected` | A recurrence was rejected. |
| `expired` | A recurrence was expired. |
| `cancelled` | A recurrence was cancelled. |
