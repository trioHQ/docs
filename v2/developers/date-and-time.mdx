---
title: "Date and time"
description: "Date and time formats used by the V2 API: UTC timestamps and plain dates."
---

All dates and times the API accepts and returns are in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) and follow [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601), a standard that is both human-readable and machine-parsable. Timestamps carry a time component; some fields are calendar dates without one.

## Timestamps

Timestamps follow the pattern `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.ffffffZ`:

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `YYYY` | 4-digit year |
| `MM` | 2-digit month (01-12) |
| `DD` | 2-digit day of the month (01-31) |
| `T` | Separator between the date and time components |
| `HH` | 2-digit hour (00-23) |
| `mm` | 2-digit minute (00-59) |
| `ss` | 2-digit second (00-59) |
| `ffffff` | Fraction of a second, returned with microsecond precision and optional in what you send |
| `Z` | The date and time are in UTC |

For example, `inserted_at` and `updated_at` come back as `2026-10-07T20:16:02.471297Z`: October 7, 2026 at 20:16:02.471297 UTC. Query filters such as `from_datetime` and `to_datetime` take the same format, for instance `2026-10-01T00:00:00Z`.

## Dates without time

Some fields are calendar dates with no time component and follow the pattern `YYYY-MM-DD`, for example `2026-10-25`. They are the business dates of a receivable or a payment:

- the `due_date` of a boleto or of the first payment of a recurrence;
- the `start_date` and `end_date` of a recurrence;
- the `expiration_date` and `issue_date` of a boleto;
- the `date` of each discount in `due_detail.discount.fixed_until_due.amounts`.
