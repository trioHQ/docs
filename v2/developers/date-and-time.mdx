---
title: "Date & Time"
description: "Date and time formats used by the V2 API: UTC timestamps and plain dates."
---

All dates and times managed and returned by our API are represented in the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) timezone and adhere to the ISO 8601 format. [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) is an internationally recognized standard for representing date and time in a human-readable and machine-parsable manner.

### **Format**

The ISO 8601 format for date and time representation follows this pattern:

`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.ffffffZ`

Where:

- YYYY represents the 4-digit year
- MM represents the 2-digit month (01-12)
- DD represents the 2-digit day of the month (01-31)
- T is the separator between the date and time components
- HH represents the 2-digit hour (00-23)
- mm represents the 2-digit minute (00-59)
- ss represents the 2-digit second (00-59)
- ffffff represents the fraction of a second, returned with microsecond precision and optional in what you send
- Z indicates that the date and time are in the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)

#### **Example**

An example of a date and time represented in the ISO 8601 format, as returned in `inserted_at` and `updated_at`:

`2026-10-07T20:16:02.471297Z`

This represents the date and time: October 7, 2026, 20:16:02.471297 (UTC). Query filters such as `from_datetime` and `to_datetime` take the same format: `2026-10-01T00:00:00Z`.

### **Dates without time**

Some fields are calendar dates with no time component and follow the pattern `YYYY-MM-DD`, for example `2026-10-25`. They are the business dates of a receivable or a payment, such as the `due_date` of a boleto or of the first payment of a recurrence, the `start_date` and `end_date` of a recurrence, the `expiration_date` and `issue_date` of a boleto, and the `date` of each discount in `due_detail.discount.fixed_until_due.amounts`.
