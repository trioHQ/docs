## Reporting an issue

When you report a bug or unexpected behavior, include as much context as you can:

- the environment (`sandbox` or `production`) and the API version you call (`/v2/...` paths);
- the `X-Request-Id` of the failed request (see [Request identification](/v2/developers/request-identification));
- request payloads and the error messages you received;
- transaction identifiers, for instance the `id` of the in or out document, and the `external_id`;
- webhook payloads;
- approximate timestamps.

Detailed reports let the team investigate and resolve issues faster.

## What happens next

1. The support team registers your case.
2. The appropriate internal team reviews the request.
3. You receive a follow-up as soon as possible.

The 24/7 support team maintains an average response and resolution time of less than 5 minutes from the moment the request is submitted.
