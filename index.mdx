Trio is a complete banking solution for companies, designed to automate financial operations, move money at scale and manage corporate treasury. Trio is authorized by the Central Bank of Brazil (No. 619, ISPB 49931906) and exposes its infrastructure through a REST API, so you can embed Pix, boleto, payments and corporate cards directly in your product.

It walks you from your first sandbox request to a production integration, and it is organized so that each section answers one kind of question.

## How the documentation is organized

- **Get Started** explains the basics every request depends on: environments, authentication, the core objects, error handling, pagination, dates and rate limits, plus how webhooks work.
- **Guides** are task-oriented walkthroughs grouped by what you want to do: onboard customers, receive money (Pix, boleto, Pix Automático, fees, split payments), pay money, run operations, connect an AI agent through [Trio MCP](/v2/mcp/what-is-trio-mcp) and use the Trio SDK.
- **API reference** is the complete catalog of V2 endpoints, generated from the OpenAPI specification, with request and response schemas and an interactive playground.
- **FAQ** collects the questions integrators ask most, about the sandbox, credentials, webhooks and boletos.

## What changed in V2

If you integrated with the V1 API, these are the differences you will meet first. Each guide also carries a short "Coming from V1?" note where it matters.

| Area | V1 | V2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paths | `/banking/...`, `/webhooks` | Every path starts with `/v2` |
| Accounts | Bank accounts and virtual accounts (`virtual_account_id`) | Bank accounts only. Every receivable and payment names its `bank_account_id` |
| Transactions | Collecting and payment documents | In documents, out documents and transfer documents under `/v2/documents`, each with its refunds |
| Response shapes | Flat ids and timestamps (`counterparty_id`, `origin_type`, `timestamp`) | Nested objects (`counterparty`, `origin`, `reference`, `fee`) and `inserted_at` / `updated_at`. Money is `{ "amount", "currency" }` |
| Pix payouts | One endpoint with `reference` | An asynchronous endpoint with `reference`, or a check-then-pay flow with `check_id`. Payments to a bank account send a `counterparty` |
| Boleto and Pix Automático | Flat discount, fine and interest fields | Structured `due_detail` rules with a `calculation_type` and percentages in basis points |
| Split payments | `split` | `splits`, with the same items |
| Updates and cancellations | Returned the object | Return `204 No Content`; read the resource back or wait for the webhook |
| Validation errors | `400` | `422`, with the rule code in `error_code` |
| Pagination | `has_next` | `has_more`, with `before` and `after` cursors |
| Webhooks | One payload version | `api_version` chooses `v100` or `v200` payloads; webhooks can be limited to an entity or a group |

Pix biometrics and virtual accounts have no V2 endpoints.
