---
title: "Fees"
description: "Charge a fee on the payment of a receivable and keep the margin above your Trio cost"
---

A **fee** is an extra value charged on the payment of a receivable. It is added on top of the receivable amount, so the payer settles both the amount and the fee when paying the Dynamic QR Code, Static QR Code, or boleto.

> All examples use fictitious data.

---

## How it is configured

The fee is set **inside the `options` object** of the create request, as an integer in **cents**.

```json
"options": {
  "fee": 150
}
```

```text
0    → no fee
150  → R$ 1.50
1000 → R$ 10.00
```

It applies the same way across:

| Receivable | Create endpoint |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Dynamic QR Code | `POST /banking/cashin/pix/qrcodes` |
| Static QR Code | `POST /banking/cashin/pix/qrcodes/static` |
| Boleto | `POST /banking/cashin/boletos` |

---

## How the fee is settled

Every receivable settled through Trio has a **processing cost** — the fee you pay to Trio for that receivable.

The fee you charge on the receivable **must be greater than your Trio processing cost**. When the payer pays, Trio keeps its own processing cost out of the fee and passes the **remaining difference on to you**.

```text
Payer pays the receivable
        │
        ├── Receivable amount ───────────────────► you
        │
        └── Fee
             │
             ├── Trio processing cost ───────────► Trio
             │
             └── Difference (fee − Trio cost) ───► you
```

In other words, the fee lets you resell the cost of collecting the receivable: the payer covers your Trio cost plus a margin, and that margin is credited to you. It works as if the payer were **subletting the receivable** from you — they pay a bit more, and the surplus above your Trio cost becomes your revenue.

<Note>
The fee must be **greater than** the Trio processing cost for that receivable. If it is not, there is no difference left to pass on to you.
</Note>

## Request example

```json
{
  "amount": 10000,
  "external_id": "qr-demo-2026-001",
  "virtual_account_id": "019f0000-1234-5678-9abc-000000000001",
  "counterparty": {
    "name": "Payer Name",
    "tax_number": "12345678901"
  },
  "description": "Pedido 12345",
  "options": {
    "allow_change_the_amount_on_payment": false,
    "show_qrcode_image": true,
    "fee": 200
  }
}
```

The fee is independent from [split payments](/guides/split-payments) — you can use either on its own or both together.
